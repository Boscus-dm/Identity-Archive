    LIMBUS COMPANY · REFERENCE

    Rules / Mechanics

    Unless explicitly stated otherwise, these mechanics follow Limbus Company. This reference documents system-specific changes and additions.

    1. Turn Structure 2. Skills and Clashing 3. Damage and Resistances 4. Sins 5. Sanity (SP) 6. Speed 7. Defensive Skills 8. Stagger 9. Critical Hits and Poise

    No rule topics match this search.

    1. Turn Structure

    Selecting Skills

    Characters have three Attack Skills: S1, S2 and S3.

    The turn rules assume those three skills, while the Identity Archive allows any number of skill rows and the E.G.O Archive records Awakening/Corrosion variants by Threadspin.

    At the beginning of the first turn, roll 1d6 twice to determine your available skills.

    RollSkill
    1–3S1
    4–5S2
    6S3

    Choose one of the two rolled skills to use for your attack. The unused skill is kept for the next turn.

    At the end of each subsequent turn:

    1. Roll 1d6 to generate a new skill.
    2. Choose between your two available skills for your next attack.
    3. Keep the unused skill and repeat the process next turn.

    Combat Manager: The draw button uses each skill's Amount as its pool weight, draws up to two different skills, and lets you queue one. A combatant can use one skill per round; after a skill is used, any unused drawn skill is kept and the pool is replenished at the next round.

    2. Skills and Clashing

    Skill Properties

    Every Attack Skill has the following properties:

    PropertyDescription
    Base / Skill PowerThe skill's starting power before Coin and other modifiers. The archives label this Skill power for Identities and Base power for E.G.O.
    Coin PowerPower added or subtracted when a Coin lands Heads, according to that Coin's type.
    Coin CountThe number of Coins used by the skill.
    Coin TypeEach Coin is Normal, Unbreakable, Excision, or Concentration.
    Damage TypeSlash, Pierce or Blunt; recorded as the skill Type or Attack type where applicable.
    Sin AffinityThe Sin associated with the skill.

    E.G.O Passive activation

    An E.G.O passive becomes permanently active for its Character after that Character uses a skill from the E.G.O once. An E.G.O record can instead be marked Always active. Both states persist when the E.G.O is unequipped and bypass its normal Threadspin unlock check; passives with Sin requirements can still become active from those requirements before either permanent state applies.

    E.G.O equipment and Threadspin

    Each managed Character may equip at most one owned E.G.O of each risk level. A Character's equipped E.G.O skills are available to all of its Identity-backed combatants only when the skill's recorded Threadspin tier is at or below the equipped Threadspin. An E.G.O passive is listed only when its recorded unlock tier is met. These are archive and Combat Manager rules; they do not model E.G.O effects beyond recorded skill costs, skills, resistances, and passive highlighting.

    Resolving Coins

    Roll 1d4 once for each Coin in your selected skill. The result determines whether that Coin lands Heads or Tails, depending on your Sanity.

    SanityTailsHeads
    01–23–4
    +25 or higher12–4
    −25 or lower1–34
    • Heads: Adds the Coin Power.
    • Tails: Does not modify the skill's Base Power.

    When using all Coins together, their power changes are cumulative. During a Clash, compare Base Power plus the active Coin's change each round.

    Sanity values between the specified thresholds do not yet have a defined d4 conversion.

    Resolving Clashes

    A Clash occurs when two opposing Attack Skills target one another.

    Guard and Counter skills participate in a Clash only when marked Clashable on the Identity. Manual Guard or Counter actions are not Clashable.

    1. Both participants roll their last available Coin; compare Base Power plus that Coin's result.
    2. The lower result loses that Coin. Normal Coins break; Unbreakable and Concentration Coins crack unless the winner used Excision.
    3. Repeat while both skills have unbroken Coins. A tie stops the Clash without an attack.
    4. The winner attacks with every unbroken or cracked Coin left. Each remaining Coin deals the skill's rank in damage dice; cracked Coins deal half their damage, rounded down.

    Unbreakable Coins crack instead of breaking and remain available for the attack at half damage. Excision Coins break an Unbreakable or Concentration Coin when they win against it. Concentration Coins are Unbreakable, automatically crit when cracked, and grant shield equal to at least 1 based on a rerolled Coin's power (Base Power plus its Coin change).

    A skill that does not enter a Clash performs a one-sided attack, resolving its damage and effects without opposition.

    Offense Level

    Offense Level provides the +X damage bonus.

    A difference of 5 Offense Levels may also provide 1 additional Clash Power in favour of the character with the higher value. This interaction is provisional and requires further testing.

    The archive has no Offense Level field, so the Combat Manager does not currently calculate this bonus. Skill Flat Damage is separate and applies only when explicitly recorded on that skill.

    3. Damage and Resistances

    Damage

    Damage is rolled separately from Clash and Coin results. Each Coin remaining after a Clash attacks; each attack rolls the skill's rank in damage dice:

    SkillDamage Roll
    S11dX
    S22dX
    S33dX

    The damage die (X) is determined primarily by the character's weapon. Use D&D weapon damage as a general reference.

    Example: Knight Carmilla uses a greatsword, so she deals d12 damage.

    Important: Clash Power does not increase damage. Effects that increase Coin Power or Clash Power only improve clashing unless they explicitly state otherwise.

    Identity Defense modifier (app rule): The Combat Manager subtracts the signed Defense modifier from rolled damage before applying resistance multipliers, with damage floored at zero. This is a provisional app rule because the reference material does not define a Defense modifier formula.

    Damage Types and Resistances

    Every attack has a physical damage type:

    TypeDescription
    SlashCutting and slashing attacks.
    PierceStabbing and piercing attacks.
    BluntCrushing and blunt-force attacks.

    Both physical and Sin Resistance multipliers apply to incoming damage. In this app, physical resistance categories are recorded on Identity records and Sin resistance categories on E.G.O records. The same resistance categories are used for both:

    NameValue (Mathematical intervals)
    Fatal(x1.5, x2]
    Weak(x1, x1.5]
    Normal[x1]
    Endure[x0.75, x1)
    Ineffective (Ineff.)(x0, x0.75)
    Immune[x0]

    4. Sins

    Sin Affinities

    Attack Skills normally have a Sin Affinity. Sins determine damage interactions, E.G.O resource generation and Resonance; the Identity and E.G.O archives also allow N/A or Sinless where appropriate. There are nine Sins in this system:

    SinColourDefault Resistance
    WrathRedSet per E.G.O
    LustOrangeSet per E.G.O
    SlothYellowSet per E.G.O
    GluttonyGreenSet per E.G.O
    GloomCyanSet per E.G.O
    PrideBlueSet per E.G.O
    EnvyPurpleSet per E.G.O
    AngstBlackFatal by default
    MadnessWhiteFatal by default

    Angst and Madness are system-specific additions, not Limbus Company Sins. They are included in the Identity and E.G.O archives for later material (Purgatorio or beyond); their E.G.O Sin Resistance defaults to Fatal and can be changed.

    Sin Resources

    Using an Attack Skill generates E.G.O Resources matching its Sin Affinity; N/A or Sinless skills generate no Sin resource.

    These resources are spent to activate E.G.Os, each of which has its own resource requirements.

    Sin Resonance

    Resonance occurs when two or more skills of the same Sin Affinity are selected during the same turn; they do not have to be adjacent. Limbus Company boosts Offense/Defense Level by chain position; this system instead grants increasing Power bonuses to matching skills.

    • Resonance: Two or more selected skills share the same Sin Affinity.
    • Absolute Resonance: Three or more skills with the same Sin Affinity are directly adjacent in the speed-ordered skill chain. This provides a stronger bonus.

    Resonance can also activate effects that specifically require a certain number of matching Sins.

    5. Sanity (SP)

    Sanity

    Sanity influences the probability of rolling Heads on Coins. Sanity ranges from −45 to +45. Its exact d4 conversion is described in the Resolving Coins rules.

    Sanity typically increases through:

    • Winning Clashes.
    • Defeating enemies.

    Sanity typically decreases through:

    • Using E.G.O.
    • Losing allies.
    • Certain status effects and abilities.

    6. Speed

    Speed Rolls and Effects

    At the start of each round, the Combat Manager rolls one initiative result inside each combatant's Speed range and orders combatants from highest to lowest. Identity-backed combatants use the selected Identity's range; manual combatants can enter a numeric range such as 3–6 or High, Mid, or Low.

    CategoryRollResult
    High1d8, reroll 1s2–8
    Mid1d61–6
    Low1d6, halve and round up1–3

    High uses 2–8, Mid uses 1–6, and Low uses 1–3.

    Haste increases Speed by its Count, while Bind decreases Speed by its Potency for one turn.

    The Status Effect Archive stores and links descriptions; it does not automatically apply effects or simulate combat.

    7. Defensive Skills

    Guard, Evade, and Counter

    There are three standard defensive actions:

    SkillEffect
    GuardGenerates Shield to absorb incoming damage.
    EvadeAttempts to avoid incoming attacks using a defensive roll.
    CounterPerforms a retaliatory attack when triggered by an incoming attack.

    Guard: Shield absorbs damage before HP. Damage absorbed by Shield does not trigger Stagger thresholds.

    Evade: Successfully evading prevents the incoming attack from dealing damage.

    Counter: Performs a retaliatory attack. It does not generally prevent the incoming attack from dealing damage.

    Individual skills may modify these effects.

    8. Stagger

    Stagger Effects

    Stagger occurs when HP damage crosses one or more Stagger Thresholds while no Shield remains. Threshold percentages are recorded individually on each Identity.

    Staggered characters:

    • Cannot use skills for the remainder of the current turn and the entirety of the following turn.
    • Have their physical Resistances changed to Fatal.
    • Become significantly more vulnerable to incoming damage.

    Stagger Levels

    Crossing multiple Stagger Thresholds increases the Stagger level and its damage multiplier.

    Stagger LevelPhysical Damage Taken
    Stagger2x
    Stagger+2.5x
    Stagger++3x

    These multipliers replace normal physical Resistance while Staggered. Sin Resistance still applies separately.

    The maximum Stagger level is Stagger++.

    Stagger Thresholds

    Thresholds are expressed as percentages of maximum HP. The Identity Archive stores the exact percentages recorded for each Identity; these High/Mid/Low rows are reference patterns, not archive categories.

    CategoryStaggerStagger+Stagger++
    High40%10%—
    Mid70%40%20%
    Low65%35%15%

    9. Critical Hits and Poise

    Critical Hits

    A damage die that rolls its maximum possible value scores a critical hit, doubling that die's damage. Poise can expand the critical range; these table rules replace Limbus Company's standard critical damage modifier.

    Example: Rolling an 8 on a d8 deals 16 damage instead of 8.

    Poise Thresholds

    Poise expands your critical hit range based on its current value.

    PoiseCritical Ranged8 Example
    0Maximum result only8
    5Top 25%7+
    10Top 50%5+
    15Top 75%3+
    20Always criticalAny result

    Poise Count Consumption

    When you score a critical hit using Poise, consume Poise Count equal to the number of damage dice on the skill.

    SkillDamage DicePoise Count Consumed
    S111
    S222
    S333

    Example: An S3 dealing 3d8 damage consumes 3 Poise Count when it scores a Poise-enabled critical hit.

    This is the current table rule for Poise consumption.
